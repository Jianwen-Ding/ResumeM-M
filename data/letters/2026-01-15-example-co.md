Replace this with a letter you actually sent. It is here so the tool has
something to work from on day one: the extension drafts new letters in the
voice of the ones already in this folder, and offers the closest previous
letter to adapt when the AI is switched off.

The parts worth keeping in your own version: open with the specific thing about
the team or product that made you apply, not with "I am writing to express my
interest". Spend the middle paragraph on one piece of work from your resume,
told as a short story with the outcome attached. Close by saying what you want
to learn there, briefly.

Keep it to three or four short paragraphs. A cover letter that repeats the
resume line by line gets skimmed; one that explains a single decision you made
gets read.
